# Diary surface

Mode: Operate. Success is saving a meal from a phone with minimal typing.

First viewport: small Food diary header; selected date with previous/next and Today controls; six readable meal rows. Saved food is the content. Import suggestions sit directly underneath empty breakfast, lunch and dinner. Tapping a suggestion opens a prefilled editor for review; it never saves silently or copies symptoms.

Daily notes preserve context without forcing a symptom into a meal category. Settings contain export, source-file import and ChatGPT connection instructions. Empty categories are ordinary, not failures or incomplete goals.
